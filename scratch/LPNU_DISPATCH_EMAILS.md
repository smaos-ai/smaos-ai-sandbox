1. **Zero Financial Obligations**: The proposed collaboration carries no mutual financial or budgetary commitments under the master Memorandum of Understanding.
2. **Synthetic & Public Data Only**: All initial benchmark testing relies strictly on public datasets and synthetic fault-injection traces, requiring no Data Processing Agreements or personal data processing.
3. **Complete Intellectual Property Protection**: Pre-existing Background Intellectual Property remains 100% owned by the originating party. Specific project scopes, publication rights, and foreground research are governed by individual, project-specific Annexes signed prior to work starting.
4. **Immediate First Project (AEIB Wedge)**: We propose a zero-budget, high-impact initial activity—the independent reproduction and academic critique of the open-source **Agent Effect Integrity Benchmark (AEIB)**.
