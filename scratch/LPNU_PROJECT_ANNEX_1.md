### 1. Scope of Work (Narrow Academic Focus)

This Project Annex establishes a zero-budget, narrow academic research collaboration focused exclusively on the independent reproduction, evaluation, and critique of the open-source **Agent Effect Integrity Benchmark (AEIB)**.

The scope is strictly limited to:

* Running the open-source AEIB fault-injection harness (`agent_chaos_proxy.py`) against synthetic HTTP 504 post-write timeout fixtures.
* Measuring unconfirmed retry behavior and idempotency key handling across open-source AI orchestration frameworks in controlled lab environments.
* Co-authoring open, peer-reviewed academic benchmark reports and methodology critiques.

---

### 2. Operational Boundaries & Data Isolation

* **100% Synthetic Data**: All test scenarios use simulated API endpoints and synthetic payload data.
* **Zero Production Access**: No access to live enterprise systems, production databases, or university operational infrastructure is required or authorized.
* **No PII or Sensitive Data**: Neither party shall transmit, ingest, or process personal data or confidential records.

---

### 3. Intellectual Property & Open Science

* **Open Source Output**: The AEIB benchmark code, fault-injection scripts, and synthetic datasets are published under the open-source **Apache 2.0 / CC-BY 4.0** licenses.
* **Publication Rights**: Both Parties retain the right to co-author and publish academic findings in peer-reviewed journals and conference proceedings, subject to standard 14-day pre-publication review for accuracy.

---

### 4. Financial Terms

Under this Project Annex, neither Party bears financial obligations to the other. Each Party participates using its own existing academic resources.
