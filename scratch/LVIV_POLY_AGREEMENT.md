# AGREEMENT ON COOPERATION

### Between

**LVIV POLYTECHNIC NATIONAL UNIVERSITY (UKRAINE)**

### And

**SOVEREIGNNEXUS (CZECH REPUBLIC)**

---

**Lviv Polytechnic National University**, represented by Head of the Artificial Intelligence Department Professor Nataliya SHAKHOVSKA, operating on the basis of the University Charter, on the one side,  
and  
**SovereignNexus**, represented by Founder & Lead Architect Andrii LEUKHIN, operating on the basis of the Organization Charter, on the other side,

hereinafter together referred to as the **Parties**, guided by a mutual desire to cooperate in the fields of higher education, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and scientific research, and considering that this cooperation meets the interests of both Parties, concluded this Agreement on Cooperation (hereinafter – the **Agreement**).

---

### Article 1. Subject of the Agreement

The subject of the Agreement is joint cooperation in the following areas: 
1.1. Scientific research in distributed systems, non-deterministic agentic AI systems, formal verification, and high-performance computing.  
1.2. Academic exchange of faculty members, researchers, and students.  
1.3. Joint organization of scientific and methodological conferences, symposia, and workshops.  
1.4. Mutual exchange of scientific publications, research materials, and open-source software benchmarks.  
1.5. Implementation of joint educational, technological, and research projects.

---

### Article 2. Organization of Cooperation

2.1. To achieve the objectives of this Agreement, the Parties will organize and execute:

* Joint research on autonomous agent execution integrity, eBPF kernel isolation, and cryptographic state proofs.
* Research and pedagogical training for faculty, PhD candidates, and postgraduate students.
* Development and delivery of specialized coursework in Spec-Driven Development, Context Cartography, and Agentic Engineering.
* Integration of Layer 08 Governance Memory for high-performance computing nodes (including TOP500-class architectures).
* Joint applications for educational and scientific research grants through Ukrainian, Czech, EU (e.g., Horizon Europe), and international funding programs.
* Facilitation of scientific publication in peer-reviewed journals and international conference proceedings.

2.2. The Parties will regularly share information regarding upcoming academic events, research calls, and collaborative opportunities.  
2.3. The Parties will explore joint educational initiatives, including specialized certificate modules, Master’s, and/or postgraduate research tracks in Agentic AI Engineering.  
2.4. **Before the start of any specific cooperation measure provided for in Article 2, a separate written agreement (Project Annex) specifying and agreeing upon the details, scope, and technical parameters of the cooperation must be concluded by both Parties.**

---

### Article 3. Responsibilities of the Parties

3.1. The Parties are responsible for their respective actions in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement. If a Party is unable to fulfill its obligations, it shall immediately notify the other Party and propose an acceptable alternative solution.  
3.2. Any disputes arising during the execution of this Agreement shall be settled amicably through mutual consultation and additional written agreements.  
3.3. Any modifications, additions, or amendments to this Agreement must be agreed upon in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

---

### Article 4. Financing

4.1. **Under this Agreement, the Parties bear no mutual financial obligations.**  
4.2. Both institutions shall actively seek external funding for collaborative activities, student/staff exchanges, and research infrastructure from Ukrainian, Czech, European, and international foundations, grant programs, and public-private scientific initiatives.

---

### Article 5. Dispute Resolution and Applicable Law

5.1. The Parties agree to settle amicably any disputes or disagreements arising from the interpretation or implementation of this Agreement through direct negotiations conducted in a spirit of goodwill and cooperation.  
5.2. If the Parties fail to reach an agreement through mutual consultations, disputes shall be resolved in accordance with applicable international law and national legislation as mutually determined by the Parties.

---

### Article 6. Validity and Termination

6.1. This Agreement enters into force upon signature by authorized representatives of both Parties. It shall remain valid for a period of **five (5) years** and may be extended for subsequent five-year periods by mutual written consent.  
6.2. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing official written notice to the other Party at least **three (3) months** prior to the intended date of cancellation.  
6.3. Termination of this Agreement shall not affect ongoing activities or projects already initiated under specific Project Annexes concluded pursuant to Clause 2.4 of Article 2, which shall be completed in accordance with their scheduled work plans.

---

### Article 7. Final Provisions

7.1. This Agreement is drawn up in four (4) original copies—two in Ukrainian and two in English. All texts are identical and carry equal legal force.
