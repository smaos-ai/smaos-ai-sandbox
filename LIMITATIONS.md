# SMAOS Sandbox Limitations & Scope of Verification

## 1. Non-Certification Statement
- **Not a Formal Certification**: Running this sandbox or generating a pass score does not constitute formal certification under EU AI Act Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 or DORA Regulation (EU) 2022/2554.
- **Vendor Contribution Only**: Reports generated by this sandbox serve as technical evidence packages for institutional risk reviews, not supervisory approvals.

## 2. Cryptographic Proof Boundaries
- **Proven**: Internal Merkle-DAG consistency, Ed25519 signature validity, canonical JCS payload formatting, and time-bounded mandate execution.
- **Not Proven**: Subjective policy correctness, model capability benchmarks, or third-party hardware security module (HSM) key custody.

## 3. Standards Status
- Final Standards: RFC 8785 (JCS), RFC 6962 (Merkle Trees), RFC 3161 (TSA), Ed25519.
- Internet-Draft Alignments: `draft-mih-scitt-agent-action-capsule-04` is implemented as a design input, not a finalized IETF standard.
